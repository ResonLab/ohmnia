Windows · local installation

Your data never leaves your machine

Ohmnia keeps all of your records in a local file that you control. There is no implicit synchronisation, no usage statistics sent anywhere, and no remote access to your accounts.

  • Local storage. A database file on your own disk, backed up like any other file.
  • No account. No sign-up, no remote authentication.
  • No telemetry. The only possible network access is the update check — disabled by default, and pointed at an address you configure yourself.
  • Works offline. Every management feature stays available without a connection.

Features

The everyday tools of a self-employed business, with no dependency on an online service.

01

Invoices and quotes

Create quotes and invoices, convert in one click, export a PDF ready to send.

02

Payment reminders

Due dates tracked and unpaid invoices chased according to your own terms.

03

Time tracking

Hours recorded per client and per project, carried straight over to an invoice line.

04

Inventory

Items and stock movements, with low-stock alerts and automatic decrements.

05

Simplified accounting

Income and expense journal, bank statement import (CSV and CAMT.053) and automatic matching.

06

Encrypted backups

Encrypted archives of your data, on the storage of your choice.

07

Multi-country

Tax settings and legal wording for Switzerland, France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Germany — starting points to be checked, not a certification.

08

Open export

Your entries, clients and items exportable to CSV and PDF at any time.
